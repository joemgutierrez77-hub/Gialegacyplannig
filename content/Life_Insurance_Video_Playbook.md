# GIA Legacy Planning — Life Insurance Video Playbook
**Purpose:** Client-lead videos only. Every video sells or serves life insurance. No recruiting, no "day in the life," no income flexing.
**Default mode:** Faceless. No one has to be on camera. (Agents who want to show their face can, using the same scripts.)
**Lever:** Produce more — more appointments from content, so agents aren't 100% dependent on bought leads.

---

## 1. Competitive Analysis — What's Out There and Where the Gap Is

| Who | What they post | What works | Their weakness = our opening |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IUL / "be your own bank" creators** | 401(k)-bashing, "tax-free retirement," "zero loss" claims | Huge views, strong curiosity hooks | Regulators and press (NerdWallet, InsuranceNewsNet) are calling it misleading. Viewers are getting skeptical. **We win by being the honest explainer.** |
| **IMO agent accounts** (SFG, FFL, etc.) | Recruiting, commission screenshots, "I made $X this month" | Gets engagement from *other agents* | It attracts recruits, not clients. A family looking for coverage scrolls right past. **We talk to families, not agents.** |
| **Direct-to-consumer brands** (Ethos, Policygenius, Bestow) | Paid UGC ads, "get a quote in 10 minutes," simple explainers | Polished, simple, short | No one is behind the brand, no follow-up, no help with the claim. **We win on "a real licensed person helps you, start to finish."** |
| **Generic agent accounts** | Stock quotes, "call me for a quote" graphics | Cheap to make | Zero value, zero reach. **We beat them on hooks and teaching.** |

### Market facts to build videos on (LIMRA / Life Happens Insurance Barometer)
- **38% of U.S. adults (~92 million)** have a life insurance need gap in 2026. That's a lot of people who need coverage.
- People **overestimate the cost of term by ~5–6×** (adults under 31). **This is the #1 hook in the playbook.** Cost myth-bust videos hit the main reason people don't buy.
- **62%** use social media when researching financial or insurance products, and **46%** say it's important that advisors connect with them there. (LIMRA, possibly the 2025 study; check the year before quoting it on screen.)
- **Hispanic adults:** only **43%** have coverage, **53%** say they need it or need more (2024), and a top reason for no coverage is "no agent ever approached me." In Texas, **Spanish versions of these videos are a wide-open lane.**

### Our position (one line, use it everywhere)
> **"Plain-English life insurance from a licensed agent. No hype, no pressure."**

Every video should leave the viewer thinking: *"That was the clearest explanation I've seen, and a real person will help me."*

## 2. Five Faceless Formats (nobody on camera)

| # | Format | How to shoot it (phone only) | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1** | **Text-on-screen + B-roll** | Film 5–8 sec clips of a kitchen table, kids' shoes by the door, keys, a house, a coffee cup. Big bold text overlay. Trending audio or calm music. | Myth-busts, stats, hooks |
| **F2** | **Hands + Whiteboard / Notepad** | Phone overhead, film only hands writing the math ("$500K term, age 35 ≈ ?"). Voiceover. | Cost math, "how much do I need" |
| **F3** | **Screen-record walkthrough** | Screen-record a calculator, a needs worksheet, or a slide. Voiceover. | Explainers (term vs. whole, living benefits) |
| **F4** | **Voiceover story** | Calm B-roll + voiceover telling an anonymized client story. | Testimonials, MP, final expense |
| **F5** | **Comment-reply text video** | Use TikTok's "reply to comment" sticker, answer over B-roll or text. | FAQs, objections, engagement |

**Voice option:** If an agent doesn't want to use their own voice, use the platform's built-in text-to-speech. **Never** use an AI avatar that looks like a real person, and never imply the voice belongs to someone it doesn't.

**Gear:** Phone + $15 phone stand + natural window light. That's it.

## 4. 12 Ready-to-Shoot Scripts (30–45 sec each)

> Format code = which faceless format to use. Swap `[KEYWORD]` for the DM keyword you're tracking (see Section 6).

### Script 1 — "You're Overpaying in Your Head" (Myth-bust · F2)
**[ON SCREEN: "How much do you THINK life insurance costs?"]**
Most people guess life insurance costs a few hundred bucks a month.
Research shows younger adults guess about five to six times the real price.
For a lot of healthy people in their 30s, a solid term policy can cost about what you spend on streaming.
Your price depends on your age, health, and coverage amount, so let's find yours.
**[ON SCREEN: "Comment QUOTE and I'll send you your real number"]**

### Script 2 — "Your Job's Life Insurance Isn't Enough" (Explainer · F1)
**[ON SCREEN: "Got life insurance through work? Watch this."]**
Three things most people don't know about work coverage:
One, it's often one or two times your salary, and families usually need more than that.
Two, if you leave or lose the job, the coverage usually goes with it.
Three, you can't always take it with you.
Work coverage is a good start. It usually isn't the whole plan.
**[ON SCREEN: "DM [KEYWORD] for a free coverage check"]**

### Script 3 — "Mortgage Protection in 30 Seconds" (MP · F1)
**[ON SCREEN: "Just bought a house? Read this."]**
You just signed for a 30-year mortgage. Here's the question nobody at closing asked:
If something happened to you, could your family keep the house?
Mortgage protection is life insurance built around your mortgage, so your family has money to stay in the home.
Many plans also include living benefits, which can pay out if you get seriously ill.
**[ON SCREEN: "New homeowner? Comment HOME"]**

### Script 4 — "Term vs. Whole Life on a Napkin" (Explainer · F2)
**[ON SCREEN: "Term vs. Whole Life — the 30-second version"]**
Term is like renting: low cost, covers you for a set number of years, like 20 or 30.
Whole life is like owning: costs more, lasts your whole life, and can build cash value.
Neither one is "best." The right one depends on your goals and budget.
I'll show you both side by side, no pressure.
**[ON SCREEN: "Comment COMPARE"]**

### Script 5 — "Living Benefits: Life Insurance You Use While Alive" (Explainer · F3)
**[ON SCREEN: "Life insurance that can pay you while you're ALIVE?"]**
Many modern policies include living benefits.
That means if you're diagnosed with a qualifying critical, chronic, or terminal illness, you may be able to access part of your death benefit early.
That money can help cover bills while you recover.
Not every policy has it, and the rules vary by carrier, so ask before you buy.
**[ON SCREEN: "DM LIVING to see if yours has it"]**

### Script 6 — "How Much Do I Actually Need?" (Math · F2)
**[ON SCREEN: "The DIME method — how much life insurance you need"]**
D: Debt. Add up your car, cards, and loans.
I: Income. Multiply your income by the years your family would need it.
M: Mortgage. What's left on the house?
E: Education. What would your kids' school cost?
Add it up. That's your starting number.
**[ON SCREEN: "Want me to run yours? Comment DIME"]**

### Script 7 — "The Story of the Kitchen Table" (Client story · F4, use with permission)
**[ON SCREEN: "She almost said 'we'll do it next year.'"]**
*(Template — fill the brackets with a REAL client story. Never invent one.)*
A [young mom] we helped said they'd deal with life insurance "next year."
We sat down, ran the numbers, and found a plan that fit their budget.
[What happened later, e.g. "Months later, her husband got seriously ill, and their living benefits helped."]
Next year isn't promised. Let's look at your plan today.
**[ON SCREEN: "DM PLAN"]**
> ⚠️ Use only real stories with written client permission. Change names and details. Don't state dollar amounts paid unless approved.

### Script 8 — "Final Expense: The Talk Nobody Wants to Have" (Final expense · F4)
**[ON SCREEN: "The average funeral costs more than most people have saved."]**
Nobody likes talking about it. But funerals can run thousands of dollars, and families often pay out of pocket.
Final expense insurance is a smaller policy made to cover those costs, so your kids aren't stuck with the bill.
Many plans have simple health questions and no medical exam.
**[ON SCREEN: "For you or your parents? Comment FAMILY"]**
> Get the current average funeral cost from NFDA before putting a number on screen.

### Script 9 — "3 Questions to Ask Before You Buy" (Trust · F1)
**[ON SCREEN: "Ask your agent these 3 questions"]**
One: Is this term or permanent, and why that one for me?
Two: Does it include living benefits?
Three: What happens to my price if I miss a payment or want to change it later?
If your agent can't answer clearly, keep shopping.
**[ON SCREEN: "Ask me anything — DM ASK"]**

### Script 10 — "Why I Don't Promise 'Tax-Free Millions'" (Counter-position · F1)
**[ON SCREEN: "Seen those 'tax-free retirement' insurance videos?"]**
Some videos make cash-value life insurance sound like a magic money machine.
Here's the honest version: products like IULs can offer tax-advantaged growth and protection.
They also have costs, caps, and rules, and they aren't right for everyone.
I'll show you the good and the trade-offs, then you decide.
**[ON SCREEN: "Want the honest breakdown? Comment HONEST"]**
> This is our signature video. Pin it to the top of every profile.

### Script 11 — "Stay-at-Home Parents Need Coverage Too" (Explainer · F1)
**[ON SCREEN: "No paycheck = no life insurance needed? Wrong."]**
If a stay-at-home parent passed away, who would handle childcare, driving, cooking, the house?
Replacing all of that can cost a lot every year.
Coverage for a stay-at-home parent is often affordable and protects the family's whole routine.
**[ON SCREEN: "Comment PARENT for a quick quote"]**

El precio depende de tu edad, tu salud y la cobertura.
Te ayudo a encontrar tu número real, sin presión.
**[EN PANTALLA: "Comenta PRECIO y te mando tu cotización"]**

## 6. From View to Appointment (the lead system)

**The path:**
`Video → comment/DM keyword → auto-reply with Calendly link → 15-min call → appointment`

**Setup (once per agent):**
1. **Profile bio:** "Licensed Life Insurance Agent · Plain-English, no pressure · Book below 👇" + **Calendly link**.
2. **DM keyword auto-reply** (use TikTok's / Instagram's built-in auto-reply):
   > "Hey! Thanks for reaching out 🙌 Here's my calendar for a free 15-min coverage check: [Calendly link]. Or reply with a good time and I'll call you."
3. **Track every lead** in the **Microsoft List: "Content Leads"** with columns: Date · Name · Platform · Keyword · Video # · Booked (Y/N) · App (Y/N).
4. **Power Automate (optional, Joe/admin builds once):** when a Calendly booking comes in, add a row to "Content Leads" and post a notice in the agent's Slack channel.

**Reply rule:** Answer every comment and DM **within 2 hours** during business hours. A fast reply is the best way to get the appointment booked.

## 8. Scorecard & Math (replace assumptions with your real numbers after 30 days)

| Metric | Target | Red flag |

| Videos posted / week | 5 | < 3 |
| Avg. views / video (after month 2) | 1,000+ | < 300 |

**Per agent, per month, at target (estimates, not promises):**
- 20 videos × 1,000 views = **20,000 views**
- 20,000 × 0.2% = **40 DMs**
- 40 × 40% = **16 appointments**
- 16 × 30% = **~5 apps/month** from content

**Agency math:** If 10 agents run this system and hit these numbers, that's about **48 extra apps/month** that don't cost any lead spend. That's roughly the **2 apps/week "active producer" standard** for about 5–6 more agents.

## 9. Compliance Guardrails (check every video against these before posting)

- ✅ Say **"tax-advantaged,"** never "tax-free."
- ✅ Use **"may," "can," "depending on your plan."** Never guarantee returns, approval, prices, or payouts.
- ✅ No fear tactics. Frame protection as care, not doom.
- ✅ Client stories need **written permission** first. Change names and details.
- ✅ Don't name a carrier or quote an exact price on screen unless approved by SFG/carrier marketing compliance.
- ✅ Keep 401(k)/retirement comparisons educational. **Never tell viewers to stop funding a 401(k).**
- ✅ Add the agent's name + "Licensed Insurance Agent" in the profile, and include any disclosures SFG requires.
- ✅ **Before an agent's first post:** send their first 5 videos through SFG marketing compliance review. Confirm the current SFG social media policy. This playbook is not legal or compliance advice.
